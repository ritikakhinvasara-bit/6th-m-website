ABOUT

6th~M is a design practice focused on making brands feel consistent as they scale.

​

Led by Ritika Khinvasara, a designer with experience across graphic and product design, the work goes beyond one-off assets, building visual systems that carry across social, decks, marketing, and web.

​

Over the years, 6th M has worked with early-stage startups, nonprofits, and e-commerce brands, as well as global organisations like UNICEF and World Bank.

​

6th~M also works with makers who need design that keeps pace with production, building the visual systems that carry a product from the first farmers market table to a regional distributor's catalogue.
