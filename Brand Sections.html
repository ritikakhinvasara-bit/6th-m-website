About

6th~M is a design practice focused on making brands feel consistent as they scale.

Led by Ritika Khinvasara, a designer with experience across graphic and product design, the work goes beyond one-off assets — building visual systems that carry across social, decks, marketing, and web.

Over the years, 6th~M has worked with early-stage startups, nonprofits, and e-commerce brands, as well as global organisations like UNICEF and the World Bank.

That range brings a balance of speed, adaptability, and an understanding of how design needs shift across different contexts.
